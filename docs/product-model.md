# Product model

## Vision

Intergrid combines discovery, editable design, and reusable implementation. Its reference points are Mobbin, Figma, GitHub, CodePen, and a curated design archive. The product should have its own identity.

The long-term goal is an infrastructure layer for reusable digital interface design. A person can find an interface, understand it, edit it, install its parts, and ship a product.

## Primary objects

| Object | Purpose | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset | A reusable piece of an interface. | Buttons, cards, navigation, hero sections, forms, animation, typography, layout. |
| Experience | A complete interface. | Landing page, SaaS product, portfolio, store, mobile app, dashboard. |
| Lab | An editable workspace based on an asset or experience. | A saved visual experiment or a reusable remix. |

The product should let a person move from an Experience to its Assets, then open either in a Lab.

## Standard item metadata

Every published object should have a consistent record. The proposed fields are title, description, category, tags, author, version, created and updated dates, compatibility, license, dependencies, technology, responsive behavior, accessibility, components, variants, and interactions.

For example, an *Aurora Dashboard* Experience could describe its React, Tailwind, and Framer Motion stack. It could list 12 components, four interactions, three responsive states, two themes, and a production-ready status. Those counts and labels are examples, not current catalog data.

## Community and curation

Community actions should center on the work: publish, save, fork, remix, comment, follow, collect, and share. A creator profile can show published interfaces, Labs, components, collections, and remixes.

The proposed curation labels serve different discovery needs:

- **Curated:** Human-selected work.
- **Experimental:** Unusual or exploratory interfaces.
- **Production:** Interfaces suitable for product use.
- **Essentials:** Foundational patterns.

The library needs quality checks and human review where needed. Contribution and review are described in [Discovery and library](discovery-library.md#contribution-flow).

## Business model to explore

Keep basic discovery accessible. The proposed tiers are:

| Tier | Possible capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Explore, save, basic Labs, and community assets. |
| Pro | Advanced and private Labs, exports, AI editing, premium systems, version history, and team workspaces. |
| Creator | Publishing, premium system sales, analytics, creator profiles, and revenue share. |
| Team | Shared libraries, private collections, design systems, permissions, and organization workspaces. |

A later marketplace could offer UI systems, design systems, components, templates, animations, and complete Experiences. Pricing, access rules, and marketplace policy are undecided.
